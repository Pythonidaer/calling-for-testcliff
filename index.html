One hockey name.

Guess the first and last name of a male NHL player from a U.S. or Canadian NHL team. Spaces, hyphens, and punctuation are already shown. Accents use their plain letters.

  1. Each wrong letter adds a part: head, body, two arms, two legs.
  2. Get three correct letters in a row to earn a hat-trick bonus: one extra miss. Once per round.
  3. With a bonus, your sixth miss is saved. The final leg appears on miss seven.

Decades use the season’s start year: 1989–90 belongs to the 1980s. Historical pools contain players with recorded regular-season appearances for that team. Current roster is a dated NHL roster snapshot. In the iOS app, it updates when a newer app build is installed. Defunct and renamed teams keep their historical names.

Use the onscreen letters or your keyboard. Choose your decade and team before pressing Play. Giving up or leaving a round after guessing counts as a loss. Scores save on this device.
