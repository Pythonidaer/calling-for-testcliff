How to play

Guess the first and last name of an NHL player. Spaces and punctuation are shown. Accents use their plain letters.

  1. Choose a decade and team, then press Play.
  2. Select letters to reveal the name. Each wrong letter draws another part of the figure.
  3. You have six misses. Three correct letters in a row earn one extra miss, once per round.

Giving up or leaving after a guess counts as a loss. Streak is your consecutive wins; Best streak is your longest winning streak. Scores save on this device.

Roster information

Historical players

Players are included when they have a recorded regular-season appearance for the selected team. Defunct and renamed teams keep their historical names.

Decades

Decades use the season’s start year. For example, the 1989–90 season belongs to the 1980s.

Current roster

This is a dated roster snapshot. An installed iOS app receives newer roster data with an app update.
