How to play

Guess the first and last name of an NHL player. Spaces and punctuation are shown. For accented letters, choose the letter without the accent. For example, choose E for É in José.

  1. Choose a decade and team, then press Play.
  2. Select letters to reveal the name. Each wrong letter adds a part to the hangman: a head, a body, two arms, and two legs.
  3. You have six misses. Three correct letters in a row earn one extra miss, once per round.

Giving up counts as a loss. Exit to Home Screen clears the unfinished round without counting a loss. Closing the app does not record a loss. The unfinished round is not saved after the app restarts.

Streak counts your consecutive wins. Best streak is your longest winning streak. Scores save on this device.

Roster information

Historical players

Players are included when they have a recorded regular-season appearance for the selected team. Defunct and renamed teams keep their historical names.

Decades

Decades use the season’s start year. For example, the 1989–90 season belongs to the 1980s.

This is a dated roster snapshot. An installed iOS app receives newer roster data with an app update.
